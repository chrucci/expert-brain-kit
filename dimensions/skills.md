# Skills

The skills the humans on a team need, gain or lose when they work with coding agents, and how those skills are built.

## Where the experts agree
- As agents take over implementation, the human skills that gain value are judgment, design and decomposition, careful review, and deep knowledge of the domain and customers. Engineers can stop memorizing API details, but they still need firm fundamentals to direct and verify agents. ([Thoughtworks: Developer value shifts to thinking, and understanding must keep pace](../principles/thoughtworks/developer-value-shifts-to-thinking-and-understanding-must-keep-pace.md), [Gergely Orosz: Hire and grow for judgment, deep expertise and domain knowledge](../principles/orosz/hire-and-grow-for-judgment-deep-expertise-and-domain-knowledge.md), [Addy Osmani: Build and protect engineering judgment on purpose](../principles/osmani/build-and-protect-engineering-judgment-on-purpose.md), [Anthropic engineering: Staff for domain and product expertise, not throughput](../principles/anthropic/staff-for-domain-and-product-expertise-not-throughput.md), [Simon Willison: Agents amplify experience, and the skill is built by visible practice](../principles/willison/agents-amplify-experience-and-the-skill-is-built-by-visible-practice.md), [Andrej Karpathy: Outsource thinking, not understanding](../principles/karpathy/outsource-thinking-not-understanding.md), [Martin Fowler: Domain knowledge and customer collaboration matter more](../principles/fowler/domain-knowledge-and-customer-collaboration-matter-more.md))
- Skill with coding agents varies widely, and people learn it through deliberate hands-on practice that leaders pay for and take part in. That means scheduled time, low-risk projects and openly sharing what works. ([Kent Beck: Learn a new technology by using it heavily](../principles/beck/learn-a-new-technology-by-using-it-heavily.md), [Gergely Orosz: Build agent skill through hands-on practice, leaders included](../principles/orosz/build-agent-skill-through-hands-on-practice-leaders-included.md), [DORA: Build AI skill through scheduled, low-risk practice](../principles/dora/build-ai-skill-through-scheduled-low-risk-practice.md), [Simon Willison: Agents amplify experience, and the skill is built by visible practice](../principles/willison/agents-amplify-experience-and-the-skill-is-built-by-visible-practice.md), [Steve Yegge: Train whole teams together on their real work](../principles/yegge/train-whole-teams-together-on-their-real-work.md))
- Agents take over the routine work that used to build understanding. That puts juniors most at risk and lets teams build up code nobody understands (cognitive debt). Teams have to create learning paths on purpose and check that people can still explain the code they ship. ([Kent Beck: Replace the starter work juniors used to learn on](../principles/beck/replace-the-starter-work-juniors-used-to-learn-on.md), [Mitchell Hashimoto: Delegate some work and keep doing the rest by hand to protect skills](../principles/hashimoto/delegate-some-work-and-keep-doing-the-rest-by-hand-to-protect-skills.md), [Addy Osmani: Build and protect engineering judgment on purpose](../principles/osmani/build-and-protect-engineering-judgment-on-purpose.md), [Thoughtworks: Developer value shifts to thinking, and understanding must keep pace](../principles/thoughtworks/developer-value-shifts-to-thinking-and-understanding-must-keep-pace.md), [Unmesh Joshi: Guard against cognitive debt from generated abstractions](../principles/joshi/guard-against-cognitive-debt-from-generated-abstractions.md))

## Where they disagree
- **How much of the real production work should engineers hand to agents?**
  - Hold back on purpose. Hand agents only the tasks they reliably do well, use them freely for throwaway prototypes, and keep doing the rest by hand so skills keep growing. ([Mitchell Hashimoto: Delegate some work and keep doing the rest by hand to protect skills](../principles/hashimoto/delegate-some-work-and-keep-doing-the-rest-by-hand-to-protect-skills.md), [Mitchell Hashimoto: Hire for knowing where AI tools help, and use them for throwaway prototypes](../principles/hashimoto/hire-for-knowing-where-ai-tools-help-and-use-them-for-throwaway-prototypes.md))
  - Push hard. Give engineers the best models and goals that look impossible, and staff with people who can run several agent sessions at once. ([Gergely Orosz: Build agent skill through hands-on practice, leaders included](../principles/orosz/build-agent-skill-through-hands-on-practice-leaders-included.md), [Anthropic engineering: Staff for domain and product expertise, not throughput](../principles/anthropic/staff-for-domain-and-product-expertise-not-throughput.md))
  - Evidence to settle it: A comparison over several months of engineers at heavy versus selective delegation on the same kinds of services, tracking both delivery and skill retention.
  - Metric: For each engineer: the share of production tasks delegated, plotted against throughput, defect rate and scores on regular debugging and code-reading checks.
- **Is agent skill best built by structured team training or by individual exploration?**
  - Train whole teams together, manager included, on their real work. Individual opt-in and other shortcuts did not stick. ([Steve Yegge: Train whole teams together on their real work](../principles/yegge/train-whole-teams-together-on-their-real-work.md))
  - Give individuals time and freedom to explore and try many tools, and teach them how to find answers rather than handing answers out. ([Kent Beck: Learn a new technology by using it heavily](../principles/beck/learn-a-new-technology-by-using-it-heavily.md), [DORA: Build AI skill through scheduled, low-risk practice](../principles/dora/build-ai-skill-through-scheduled-low-risk-practice.md))
  - Evidence to settle it: Run both formats across comparable teams and compare how many people are still using agents on real tasks some weeks later.
  - Metric: Share of trainees still regularly using agents (single-agent or multi-agent) six weeks after team-based training versus self-directed exploration time.

## Measured
- In a controlled study, engineers learning new material by delegating to AI scored 50% on a comprehension quiz against 67% for engineers who coded by hand, with no real speed gain. The biggest gap was on debugging questions. Engineers who used the AI to ask conceptual questions and get explanations kept their mastery. ([Anthropic engineering: Protect the skills humans need to oversee agents](../principles/anthropic/protect-the-skills-humans-need-to-oversee-agents.md), [Addy Osmani: Build and protect engineering judgment on purpose](../principles/osmani/build-and-protect-engineering-judgment-on-purpose.md))
- About five hours of agentic coding training, given to one whole team at a time with the manager present and using the team's own work, led to lasting adoption. Shorter classes, larger groups and individual opt-in did not produce the same results. ([Steve Yegge: Train whole teams together on their real work](../principles/yegge/train-whole-teams-together-on-their-real-work.md))
- Agent session success, measured as verified success, abandonment and output per prompt, tracked the operator's domain expertise more than raw coding throughput. ([Anthropic engineering: Staff for domain and product expertise, not throughput](../principles/anthropic/staff-for-domain-and-product-expertise-not-throughput.md))

## Open hypotheses
- Speedups from AI tools may only appear after hundreds of hours of use, so early results understate their value. ([METR: Treat AI tool skill as a long-term investment](../principles/metr/treat-ai-tool-skill-as-a-long-term-investment.md))
  - How to test it: For each developer, log cumulative hours with the tool and measure task speed on comparable tasks over time to see whether a speedup appears, and when.
- Hiring through a substantial project built with agents and then attacked by adversarial agents picks better agentic engineers than puzzle interviews do. ([Andrej Karpathy: Hire agentic engineers with projects that agents try to break](../principles/karpathy/hire-agentic-engineers-with-projects-that-agents-try-to-break.md))
  - How to test it: Hire some candidates through each process and compare their throughput and incident rates over their first six months.
