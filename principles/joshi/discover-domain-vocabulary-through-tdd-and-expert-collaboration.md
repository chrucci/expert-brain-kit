# Discover domain vocabulary through TDD and expert collaboration

Verified: 3 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Processes (processes)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Track how consistently domain terms are used across the codebase, the prompts and conversations with domain experts, and whether agent output drifts less as that consistency improves.

Precise domain vocabulary is found through iterative test-driven development and close work between domain experts, users and developers. Agent-assisted teams still need this feedback loop, because precise vocabulary is what lets an LLM turn intent into useful code.

## Quotes

> Techniques like TDD are excellent for this iterative development of the vocabulary. They help us discover the right names, the right abstractions, and the right boundaries by forcing continuous feedback between the model and its behavior.
>
> [What Is Code?](https://martinfowler.com/articles/what-is-code.html)

> Coding can not happen in isolation. There must be close collaboration between domain experts, users and developers.
>
> [What Is Code?](https://martinfowler.com/articles/what-is-code.html)

> But when the vocabulary is precise, consistent, and embodied in the code, the LLM can map our intent more reliably to useful implementation.
>
> [What Is Code?](https://martinfowler.com/articles/what-is-code.html)
