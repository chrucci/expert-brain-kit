# Map a model's jagged capabilities empirically

Verified: 6 verbatim quotes from 2 sources

Dimension: Tools (tools)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Keep a team eval suite of representative tasks and compare rework rates of agent changes on boilerplate versus novel work as setups and models change.

LLMs are superhuman in some areas, especially verifiable ones, and fail strangely elsewhere, and they come with no manual. Avoid both dismissal and blind trust: learn by experiment which tasks and coding modes suit them, and plan around their weaknesses instead of treating them like a human colleague.
