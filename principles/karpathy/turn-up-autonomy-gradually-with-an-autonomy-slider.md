# Turn up autonomy gradually with an autonomy slider

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Roles for agents (agent-roles)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: For each autonomy level record task complexity, intervention rate and rework, and widen autonomy only where intervention stays low.

With fallible models, build augmentations (Iron Man suits) rather than full replacements (Iron Man robots). Let people choose how much autonomy to hand over based on the task, and let automation take more over time while humans supervise from a higher level of abstraction.

## Quotes

> you are in charge of the autonomy slider and depending on the complexity of the task at hand you can uh tune the amount of autonomy that you're willing to give up uh for that task
