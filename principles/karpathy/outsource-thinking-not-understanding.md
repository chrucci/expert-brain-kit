# Outsource thinking, not understanding

Verified: 3 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Skills (skills)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Assess engineers on conceptual system questions rather than API recall and check whether the scores track the quality of their agent-directed output.

Agents remember surface details such as API arguments, so memorizing those matters less. The human still needs a firm grasp of fundamentals like storage, invariants and security boundaries, because you can't direct agents well without understanding.

## Quotes

> I don't remember this stuff anymore because I don't have to. These details are handled by the intern because agents have good recall.
>
> [Sequoia Ascent 2026 summary](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/)

> But you still have to understand the fundamentals.
>
> [Sequoia Ascent 2026 summary](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/)

> Understanding is still the bottleneck because you cannot be a good director if you do not understand.
>
> [Sequoia Ascent 2026 summary](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/)
