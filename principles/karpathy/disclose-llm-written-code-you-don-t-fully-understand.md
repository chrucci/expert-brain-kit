# Disclose LLM-written code you don't fully understand

Verified: 1 verbatim quote from 1 source

Dimension: Processes (processes)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Track the share of PRs with a disclosure and compare defect or revert rates between disclosed LLM sections and the rest.

Contributors should declare which parts of a change came mostly from an LLM or which parts they don't fully understand. Reviewers then know where to look hardest, and ownership stays honest.

## Quotes

> Current AI policy: disclosure. When submitting a PR, please declare any parts that had substantial LLM contribution and that you have not written or that you do not fully understand.
>
> [nanochat: README.md](https://github.com/karpathy/nanochat/blob/master/README.md)
