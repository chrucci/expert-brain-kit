# The human owns the spec, design and taste

Verified: 6 verbatim quotes from 2 sources

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Count design-level defects (identity, security, data model) that reach review or production with and without a human-approved spec.

Treat agents like capable interns: they fill in the blanks while the human sets the spec, the system design and the judgment. Agents make subtle, plausible design mistakes, such as matching payments to users by email, and their promise to do better next time is not a fix.

## Quotes

> Right now the agents are like interns. You still have to be in charge of aesthetics, judgment, taste, and oversight.
>
> [Sequoia Ascent 2026 summary](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/)

> There is something more general: you work with your agent to design a detailed spec, maybe basically the docs, and get agents to write them. You are in charge of oversight and the top-level categories.
>
> [Sequoia Ascent 2026 summary](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/)

> So you are in charge of taste, engineering, design, and whether the system makes sense. You ask for the right things: for example, we tie everything to unique user IDs. The agents fill in the blanks.
>
> [Sequoia Ascent 2026 summary](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/)

> Around here, I caught Claude using a really bad idea approach to match up a successful Stripe payment to user credits
>
> [Vibe coding MenuGen](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/vibe-coding-menugen/)

> they make subtle but critical design mistakes when you watch them closely, and sometimes they hallucinate or gaslight you about solutions.
>
> [Vibe coding MenuGen](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/vibe-coding-menugen/)

> It thanks me for pointing out the issue and tells me that it will do it correctly in the future, which I know is just gaslighting.
