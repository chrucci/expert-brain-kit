# Measure internal quality and rework, not generation speed

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 14 sources

Dimension: Metrics (metrics)

Evidence: measurement

How to measure: Track first-pass acceptance, iteration cycles per task, post-merge rework and DORA metrics against a pre-agent baseline, and check whether throughput gains reach production without stability falling.

Code that works can still add debt and slow future work for humans and agents. Judge agent collaboration by first-pass acceptance, iteration cycles, post-merge rework, DORA stability metrics and business outcomes against a pre-AI baseline, rather than lines generated, tokens spent or time to first output.
