# Fold agents into proven collaborative practices and adopt them gradually

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 8 sources

Dimension: Processes (processes)

Evidence: measurement

How to measure: Track stories per iteration against a pre-AI baseline through each adoption stage, alongside cycle time, first-pass approval and incident rates for teams that keep pairing and peer review.

Thoughtworks credits its results to layering agents onto pairing, mobbing, shared ownership and peer review across the whole lifecycle, not to automation alone. Roll agent workflows out in phases with hands-on workshops, staged onboarding and leaders who use the tools themselves, and expect a productivity dip before the gains arrive.
