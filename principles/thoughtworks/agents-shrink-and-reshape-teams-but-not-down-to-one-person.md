# Agents shrink and reshape teams, but not down to one person

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 7 sources

Dimension: Number of people (team-size)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Compare team size and elapsed time of agent-assisted projects against their pre-agent estimates across several projects, alongside quality and incident rates for teams cut to one agent coordinator.

With agents, small teams that include analysts and people with permeable, cross-disciplinary roles have delivered work estimated for far larger teams, and big builds need a new delivery lifecycle rather than agents bolted onto the old one. A stable team still has to hold the shared mental model, so one person directing agents cannot replace a whole team.
