# Use agents to break down discipline silos

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Number of people (team-size)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Measure the share of changes shipped by or paired with non-engineers and the lead time from a design or product decision to production, before and after reorganising.

Agents let designers and product people implement their own decisions and pair across disciplines. Organise as one cross-functional team whose first loyalty is to the team, and don't use the tools to harden functional silos.

## Quotes

> the possibility both of designers and product people who make their decisions and implement them directly without the intervention of a programmer.
>
> [Nobody Knows at 00:31:51](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/nobody-knows?t=1911s)

> The augmented development tools give us a chance to break down those silos. Now they also give us tools to enforce the silos more directly. And I hope we don't do that.
>
> [Nobody Knows at 00:33:07](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/nobody-knows?t=1987s)
