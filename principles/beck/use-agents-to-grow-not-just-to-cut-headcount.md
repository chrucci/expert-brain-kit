# Use agents to grow, not just to cut headcount

Verified: 1 verbatim quote from 1 source

Dimension: Number of people (team-size)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Track output or revenue per engineer and new initiatives started while headcount stays flat after agent adoption.

Agents are not only a reason to swap salaries for agent spend. Leaders can use them to grow the business and afford innovation without adding headcount, which reframes the staffing question.

## Quotes

> How about I can grow my business without growing the cost of the head count? How about I can innovate? I can afford to innovate. There's lots of other options.
>
> [Genie Lessons: Nobody Wants Agents at 00:50:22](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/genie-lessons-nobody-wants-agents?t=3022s)
