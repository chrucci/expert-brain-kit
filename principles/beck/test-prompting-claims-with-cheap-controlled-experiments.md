# Test prompting claims with cheap controlled experiments

Verified: 4 verbatim quotes from 2 sources

Dimension: Processes (processes)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Track the share of prompt or workflow changes adopted only after a controlled comparison with a baseline, and whether gains hold up.

Don't accept prompting folklore. Agent runs are cheap, so test claims against a control and the full cross product of interventions, or rebuild the same project varying one factor at a time, to learn what actually changes the output.
