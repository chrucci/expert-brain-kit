# Try many cheap attempts and keep the winners

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 3 sources

Dimension: Techniques (techniques)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Compare delivery cost and time for best-of-N agent attempts and high-discard workflows against a single carefully prompted attempt, and track how early losing ideas are abandoned.

Maximize learning by trying many different approaches and throwing most away. In the spirit of the Bitter Lesson, letting many agents try different approaches and selecting those that ship features at lowest cost may beat hand-encoding your own expertise.
