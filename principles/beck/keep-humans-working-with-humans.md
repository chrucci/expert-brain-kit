# Keep humans working with humans

Verified: 3 verbatim quotes from 2 sources

Dimension: Processes (processes)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Count cross-person interactions per change and multi-human steering sessions, and compare stakeholder confidence, rework and incident rates against solo agent work.

When each person works alone with an agent, the team loses the small daily interactions that build trust, and today's tools let many agents share a codebase but not many people. Keep pairing and on-team customers in agent-augmented work, and treat real-time collaborative steering by several humans as the frontier worth building.

## Quotes

> Right now, five agents can work on this codebase simultaneously. Five people can’t. That’s backwards.
>
> [Genie Lessons: Nobody Wants Agents](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/genie-lessons-nobody-wants-agents)

> Everybody's focused on single player first. What if we focused on multiplayer?
>
> [Genie Lessons: Nobody Wants Agents at 00:56:16](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/genie-lessons-nobody-wants-agents?t=3376s)

> Encouraging single player development eliminates most of the little chances to build trust.
>
> [Trust Factory](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/trust-factory)
