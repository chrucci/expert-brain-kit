# A person must own the system's long-term health

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 7 sources

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Track agent task success rate, follow-on change lead time and the share of agent-built speculative structure later reworked or deleted as the codebase grows.

Coding agents are poor at keeping future options open. They build speculative structure for free, and they won't keep code readable unless told to. Better specs or agent files won't stop complexity from eventually exceeding what the agent can handle, so a human has to manage the system's structure, readability and design timing over time.
