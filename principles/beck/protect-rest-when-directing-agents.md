# Protect rest when directing agents

Verified: 1 verbatim quote from 1 source

Dimension: Emerging ideas (emerging)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Correlate hours worked by the humans directing agents with defect rates and decision reversals.

Directing agents takes intense concentration, and good software decisions come from insight in a rested mind. Leads should not turn agent speed into longer human hours.

## Quotes

> Software development is a game of insight. And insight comes to the prepared, rested mind.
