# Humans keep the caring and the accountability

Verified: 3 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Check whether every agent-produced change and every autonomous-agent incident has a named accountable human, and compare defect rates with and without an active owner.

An agent has no drive of its own to make the work good, and taking humans out of the loop doesn't remove accountability; it only hides who holds it. Teams should name the person who owns the quality and outcome of each piece of agent work.

## Quotes

> This one is about what stays with us. Who cares whether the work is good? Who is accountable when it goes wrong?
>
> [How Do You Know That?](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/how-do-you-know-that)

> A human brain never just sits there; the computer is a lump until we bring the caring. The wanting-it-to-be-good is still ours.
>
> [How Do You Know That?](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/how-do-you-know-that)

> Removing the human doesn’t remove the accountability. It just hides who holds it.
>
> [How Do You Know That?](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/how-do-you-know-that)
