# Domain knowledge and customer collaboration matter more

Verified: 1 verbatim quote from 1 source

Dimension: Skills (skills)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Measure how often engineers work directly with users, and how often agent-built features are reworked because requirements were misunderstood.

When agents write the code, programmers add more value by understanding the domain and working with users and customers to define the product step by step. Teams should move skill-building toward domain knowledge and product discovery.

## Quotes

> Additionally this raises the
>   importance of programmers understanding the domain they are working with,
>   collaborating with users and customers to iteratively define and build their
>   product.
>
> [Agentic Programming](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/AgenticProgramming.html)
