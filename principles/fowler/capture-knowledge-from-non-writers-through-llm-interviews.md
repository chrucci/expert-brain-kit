# Capture knowledge from non-writers through LLM interviews

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Skills (skills)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Measure how much of the team's knowledge is documented, and how many people contribute to it, before and after offering interview-based capture.

Team members who struggle to write can get knowledge out of their heads by letting an LLM interview them. AI-flavoured documentation is better than missing documentation, so writing skill should not be what stops someone from contributing knowledge.

## Quotes

> Maybe such
>   people would find it easier to ask an LLM to interview them than to write a
>   document themselves.
>
> [Interrogatory LLM](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/InterrogatoryLLM.html)

> Certainly the result will have that tang of AI-writing
>   that folks like me shudder at - but that's better than not having the
>   information itself, either due to rushed writing or no writing at all.
>
> [Interrogatory LLM](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/InterrogatoryLLM.html)
