# Humans lead and govern agent crews

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 3 sources

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Track the share of engineer time spent directing and reviewing agent work, and compare features shipped with features humans actually reviewed or tried.

The human job shifts from pair-programming to engineering leadership: shepherding crews of agents and acting as the governor who paces factory output to what people can actually vet and use. Once a person curates dozens of deployments, they become the bottleneck and should build manager-level crews.
