# Train whole teams together on their real work

Verified: 3 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Skills (skills)

Evidence: measurement

How to measure: Compare the share of trainees still in the single-agent or multi-agent spend band six weeks after team-based versus individual opt-in training.

Teach agentic coding one team at a time, manager included, in about five hours of work time using the team's actual work, with a second course for multi-agent work. Shortcuts such as shorter classes, larger groups or individual opt-in did not stick.

## Quotes

> The trainees must bring their actual work, and the instructor(s) will help them learn how to do it with agents.
