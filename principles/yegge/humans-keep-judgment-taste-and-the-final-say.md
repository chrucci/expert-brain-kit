# Humans keep judgment, taste and the final say

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 5 sources

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Track harmful or rolled-back autonomous actions per day, the share of escalations reaching a human that were truly judgment calls, and how often agents re-ask settled questions.

Even strong models show poor judgment daily when unsupervised, so humans stay the final escalation tier for design and judgment. They settle the agents' questions as recorded law, make the taste call on whether a change belongs and own the work agents carry out. Only use agents where people can still verify the output.
