# Build in the open around a living prototype

Verified: 5 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Processes (processes)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Track rework caused by stale information, and compare idea-to-ship time for prototype-driven versus spec-driven work.

At agent speed, every human and agent work stream should be visible all the time, so teammates correct each other and models get the history they need to merge continuously. Teams should evolve a shared prototype in short cycles instead of writing a big upfront spec.
