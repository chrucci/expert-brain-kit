# Small, oversubscribed human teams

Verified: 4 verbatim quotes from 3 sources

Dimension: Number of people (team-size)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Track throughput per person by team size and the ratio of worthwhile open work to people.

Teams of two or three people with blurring roles are the sweet spot, and three to five engineers running agent crews can replace costly bought software. Keep teams oversubscribed with work, because when there are more people than work they fight over projects.
