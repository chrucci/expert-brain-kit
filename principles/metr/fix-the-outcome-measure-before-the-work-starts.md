# Fix the outcome measure before the work starts

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Metrics (metrics)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Compare productivity conclusions drawn from output counts such as lines of code or PRs with time-to-complete on tasks scoped before AI assignment, and check whether they diverge.

Output counts such as lines, commits or pull requests can go up with no real gain, because AI can make code more verbose or split work into smaller pieces. Define the unit of work before assigning conditions, and measure the time it takes to complete it.

## Quotes

> It’s possible for AI assistance to affect the outcomes without actually increasing productivity, e.g. by causing developers to write more verbose but functionally equivalent code, or causing them to break up pull requests into smaller chunks of work.
>
> [Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity at p.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.09089#page=3)

> Crucially, the tasks are definedbefore they are randomized, limiting the impact of effects from AI assistance unrelated to productivity (e.g., more verbose but functionally equivalent code)
>
> [Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity at p.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.09089#page=2)
