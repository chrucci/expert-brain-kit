# Treat AI tool skill as a long-term investment

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Skills (skills)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Track each developer's measured task speed with AI against their cumulative hours using the tool, to see whether and when a speedup appears.

Gains from AI tools may only appear after hundreds of hours of use, far more than most developers have logged. Don't judge a tool on early results, and budget for skill-building as an investment.

## Quotes

> It also may be the case that there are strong learning effects for AI tools like Cursor that only appear after several hundred hours of usage—our developers typically only use Cursor for a few dozen hours before and during the study.
>
> [Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/)

> they may view it as an investment into learning skills that they expect to be useful with future (more capable) systems.
>
> [Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/)
