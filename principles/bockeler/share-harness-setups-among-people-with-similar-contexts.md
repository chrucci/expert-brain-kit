# Share harness setups among people with similar contexts

Verified: 5 verbatim quotes from 2 sources

Dimension: Processes (processes)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Compare agent rework rates on shared team configurations versus personal or imported ones, and track how far team copies drift from the shared template.

Proving that an agent setup works takes time, and there are no unit tests for context engineering, so setups are worth sharing: within a team, and across an organisation as harness templates for its common stacks. Setups copied from strangers bring contradictory instructions. Shared templates bring the familiar service-template problems of forks and teams struggling to take in updates.

## Quotes

> It takes quite a bit of time to build up a good setup, because you have to use a configuration for a while to be able to say if it’s working well or not - there are no unit tests for context engineering.
>
> [Context Engineering for Coding Agents](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/context-engineering-coding-agents.html)

> The context of the sharer and the receiver has to be as similar as possible - it works a lot better inside of a team than between strangers on the internet
>
> [Context Engineering for Coding Agents](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/context-engineering-coding-agents.html)

> Harness templates allow us to share common guides and sensors around a larger software organization.
>
> [Harness Engineering - first thoughts](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/harness-engineering-memo.html)

> Will harnesses — with custom linters, structural tests, basic context and knowledge documentation, and additional context providers — become the new service templates?
>
> [Harness Engineering - first thoughts](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/harness-engineering-memo.html)

> With service templates, teams contribute back as they gain experience, then other teams often struggle to incorporate updates.
>
> [Harness Engineering - first thoughts](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/harness-engineering-memo.html)
