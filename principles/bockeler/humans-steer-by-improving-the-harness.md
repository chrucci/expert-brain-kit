# Humans steer by improving the harness

Verified: 3 verbatim quotes from 2 sources

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Track how often each class of agent mistake recurs after a control is added for it, and whether human review time shifts toward high-risk changes.

The human's job is to steer agents by turning every recurring issue into a better guide or sensor, not another manual fix. A harness can capture only part of what experienced developers bring, so it should direct human judgement to where it matters most rather than try to remove it.

## Quotes

> Harnesses attempt to externalise and make explicit what human developer experience brings to the table, but they can only go so far.
>
> [Harness Engineering - first thoughts](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/harness-engineering-memo.html)

> A good harness should not necessarily aim to fully eliminate human input, but to direct it to where our input is most important.
>
> [Harness Engineering - first thoughts](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/harness-engineering-memo.html)

> Whenever an issue happens multiple times, the feedforward and feedback controls should be improved to make the issue less probable to occur in the future, or even prevent it.
>
> [Harness engineering for coding agent users](https://martinfowler.com/articles/harness-engineering.html)
