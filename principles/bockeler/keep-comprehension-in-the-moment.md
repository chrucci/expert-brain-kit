# Keep comprehension in the moment

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Compare rework rate and late-stage defects between tasks reviewed as output arrived and tasks run with high autonomy and deferred review.

Giving strong models a lot of autonomy and deferring review often led to constant rework and late surprises. Humans should review and understand agent output as it arrives, because deferred understanding comes back as rework.

## Quotes

> My experience with giving strong models lots of autonomy, and surrendering to them in a way, has often led to constant rework and surprises at later stages.
>
> [Experiences with local models for coding](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/local-models-for-coding-experiences.html)

> So I've enjoyed that the weaker models make me go slower, and take more care in the moment, instead of deferring that comprehension and care to the future.
>
> [Experiences with local models for coding](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/local-models-for-coding-experiences.html)
