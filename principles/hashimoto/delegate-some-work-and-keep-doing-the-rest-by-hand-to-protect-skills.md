# Delegate some work and keep doing the rest by hand to protect skills

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Skills (skills)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Track how each engineer's work splits between manual and delegated tasks, and assess skill growth in each area, especially for juniors.

Give agents the tasks they reliably do well and keep doing focused manual work on the rest. People stop building skills only in what they delegate. This trade-off matters most for juniors who don't yet have strong fundamentals.

## Quotes

> Well, you're trading off: not forming skills for the tasks you're delegating to the agent while continuing to form skills naturally in the tasks you continue to work on manually.
>
> [My AI Adoption Journey](https://mitchellh.com/writing/my-ai-adoption-journey)

> The skill formation issues particularly in juniors without a strong grasp of fundamentals deeply worries me, however.
>
> [My AI Adoption Journey](https://mitchellh.com/writing/my-ai-adoption-journey)
