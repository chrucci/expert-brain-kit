# Humans own the what, agents own the how

Verified: 5 verbatim quotes from 2 sources

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: measurement

How to measure: Classify session decisions as planning or execution and track who makes each, and check that every agent workstream has a named human owner.

In practice people make most planning decisions and agents most execution decisions. Design roles around that: each workstream has a named human owner who sets goals, rules on user-visible tradeoffs and pushes agents past their default caution, while agents find problems, implement and present evidence.
