# Staff for domain and product expertise, not throughput

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 3 sources

Dimension: Skills (skills)

Evidence: measurement

How to measure: Compare verified-success and abandonment rates and agent output per prompt by operator domain expertise and coding background.

Models supply raw coding throughput; what makes agent sessions succeed is the operator's grasp of the problem domain, which multiplies how much the agent accomplishes per instruction. Hire for product sense, deep systems expertise and generalists who move across disciplines and juggle several agent sessions.
