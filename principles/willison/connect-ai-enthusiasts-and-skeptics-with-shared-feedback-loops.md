# Connect AI enthusiasts and skeptics with shared feedback loops

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Emerging ideas (emerging)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Survey both groups on speed, incidents and system understanding, compare their answers with delivery and reliability data, and track whether the gap narrows once feedback loops are in place.

Enthusiasts and skeptics on the same team see different realities: the speed gains, and the losses in reliability and knowledge. Leaders should deliberately design feedback loops that give both groups a shared, evidence-based view.
