# One person can do a team's work behind narrow interfaces

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Number of people (team-size)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Compare functionality delivered per person and time spent coordinating between component-owning individuals and a traditional team of five or six.

Martin offers this as a conjecture: one human directing agents can do the work of five or six developers. If so, teams will become individuals who each own large components and coordinate through narrow interfaces, with less collaboration and more output.

## Quotes

> This is my wild conjecture, but it seems to me that a single human can now do the work of a team.
>
> [Uncle Bob: Why I Stopped Reading Code and Building Harnesses | Aviator](https://www.aviator.co/podcast/uncle-bob-martin-ai-clean-code)

> Instead of a tight-knit group of five or six developers constantly communicating with each other, I think we're going to have isolated individuals producing much more code than before, much larger kinds of elements. And they'll have to communicate at a very narrow interface boundary.
>
> [Uncle Bob: Why I Stopped Reading Code and Building Harnesses | Aviator](https://www.aviator.co/podcast/uncle-bob-martin-ai-clean-code)
