# Smaller teams with blurring roles

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Number of people (team-size)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Track the surface area owned per engineer (services, features or domains) as agent use grows.

With agents, fewer people can cover much more surface area. The lines between engineering and management, product, business and design, and between frontend and backend, blur, so teams should be staffed for breadth.

## Quotes

> A smaller number of people can cover dramatically more surface area, which means their jobs are more fun and vital and interesting than ever before.
>
> [Happy Birthday to Us: Honeycomb 10 Year Manifesto, Part 1](https://www.honeycomb.io/blog/honeycomb-10-year-manifesto-part-1)

> Roles are blurring all over the place between eng and manager, eng and product, eng and business, eng and design, frontend eng and backend eng.
>
> [Happy Birthday to Us: Honeycomb 10 Year Manifesto, Part 1](https://www.honeycomb.io/blog/honeycomb-10-year-manifesto-part-1)
