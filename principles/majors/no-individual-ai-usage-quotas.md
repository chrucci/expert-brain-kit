# No individual AI-usage quotas

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Metrics (metrics)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Count shareable AI-practice insights per engineer per month, and check that no individual usage quota is used in performance reviews.

Use adoption metrics to understand overall usage, but assess people through their managers rather than with per-person quantitative targets. A rough heuristic, such as producing a shareable insight about once a month, keeps people experimenting instead of gaming numbers.

## Quotes

> While we may look at some metrics to understand adoption and usage, there's no specific set of quantitative metrics we expect each person to hit.
>
> [AI Norms & Values, Part 2 of 3: AI for Honeycomb Engineering](https://www.honeycomb.io/blog/ai-norms-values-part-2-ai-honeycomb-engineering)

> That said, sometimes a rough heuristic can help keep us all calibrated.
>
> [AI Norms & Values, Part 2 of 3: AI for Honeycomb Engineering](https://www.honeycomb.io/blog/ai-norms-values-part-2-ai-honeycomb-engineering)
