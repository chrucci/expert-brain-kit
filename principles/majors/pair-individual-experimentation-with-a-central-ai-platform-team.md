# Pair individual experimentation with a central AI platform team

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Emerging ideas (emerging)

Evidence: opinion

Adopting AI takes both distributed and centralised effort. Engineers are expected to keep experimenting and learning on their own, while an enablement team owns the AI platform, robust tooling and the next steps on agentic patterns.

## Quotes

> This team has a broader charter beyond AI, but we recognize that we need both distributed and centralized efforts to achieve our vision.
>
> [AI Norms & Values, Part 2 of 3: AI for Honeycomb Engineering](https://www.honeycomb.io/blog/ai-norms-values-part-2-ai-honeycomb-engineering)

> Expect the company to provide you with resources like product subscriptions, tokens, and shared best practices, but know that your own continuous experimentation and learning will also be required.
>
> [AI Norms & Values, Part 2 of 3: AI for Honeycomb Engineering](https://www.honeycomb.io/blog/ai-norms-values-part-2-ai-honeycomb-engineering)
