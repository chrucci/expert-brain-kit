# Route AI's downstream costs back to whoever claimed the win

Verified: 3 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Processes (processes)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Track the share of announced AI-driven changes whose write-ups list downstream cleanup or incidents, and whether the cleanup hours they cause go down over time.

The people who announce AI wins are often not the people who clean up after them, so the team has no natural feedback loop. Teams should report wins together with their downstream costs, in the same forum, so that everyone learns which agent-driven changes are actually cheap.

## Quotes

> The problem is, the wins and costs are happening to two different groups of people. There is no natural feedback loop.
