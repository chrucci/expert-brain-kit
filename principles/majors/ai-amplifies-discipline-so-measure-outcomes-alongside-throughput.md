# AI amplifies discipline, so measure outcomes alongside throughput

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 3 sources

Dimension: Metrics (metrics)

Evidence: measurement

How to measure: Track merged PRs per engineer next to the defect backlog, idea-to-ship lead time, change failure rate and downtime, before and after AI adoption.

Large AI gains go to teams that already have engineering discipline, short feedback loops and a habit of measuring. Speed alone is an immature definition of better, so teams should read throughput next to team-level outcomes such as defect backlog, lead time, quality and downtime.
