# Managers build context and stay hands-on

Verified: 3 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: opinion

When AI can do the work of handing out tasks, a manager's job is to build shared understanding of what the team is doing and why. Leaders should stay hands-on enough to know what shipping a change with agents feels like.

## Quotes

> Because I don't believe in a world where engineers are just given tasks. Here's your GRI. Go do the things. AI can do that.
>
> [Stop being skeptical about AI for development with Charity Majors at 01:11:24](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/stop-being-skeptical-about-ai-for?t=4284s)

> I want people who understand what we're trying to do. Understand how we're trying to do it or who are there to help us figure out how we're going to do it.
>
> [Stop being skeptical about AI for development with Charity Majors at 01:11:24](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/stop-being-skeptical-about-ai-for?t=4284s)

> You should know what it feels like to submit a diff, to get a PR through.
>
> [Stop being skeptical about AI for development with Charity Majors at 01:10:02](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/stop-being-skeptical-about-ai-for?t=4202s)
