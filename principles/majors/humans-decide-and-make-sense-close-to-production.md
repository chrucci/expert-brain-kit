# Humans decide and make sense, close to production

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 4 sources

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Measure the share of engineer time spent on specs, experiments, production validation and decisions compared with hand-writing code and searching, and how often engineers look at production telemetry outside incidents.

AI does not replace operators or engineers. Their work moves from typing code and searching for problems to writing specs, running experiments, interpreting production feedback and deciding what to do next. Engineers should live closer to production than before, so that human attention goes where judgment matters.
