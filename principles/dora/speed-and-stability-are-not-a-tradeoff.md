# Speed and stability are not a tradeoff

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Metrics (metrics)

Evidence: measurement

How to measure: Track change failure and rework rates together with deployment frequency and lead time as AI adoption grows, and check whether instability rises.

Throughput and stability improve together, and top teams do well on both. Going slower doesn't make delivery safer, and more AI-driven throughput doesn't make up for the damage instability does to products and people.
