# Build AI skill through scheduled, low-risk practice

Verified: 3 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Skills (skills)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Track scheduled time spent on AI experimentation and whether developers' trust in AI-generated code and their productivity rise afterward.

Engineers build AI skill and calibrated trust by exploring tools hands-on in sandboxes or low-risk work, with autonomy and test automation behind them. Leaders should put this learning time into the schedule instead of expecting it to happen after hours.

## Quotes

> Foster an environment that encourages continuous exploration of AI tools by dedicating time for individuals and teams to discover beneficial use cases and granting them autonomy to chart their own course.
>
> [Accelerate at p.46](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/2024_final_dora_report.pdf#page=46)

> Build trust with AI technologies through hands-on experience in sandbox or low-risk environments.
>
> [Accelerate at p.46](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/2024_final_dora_report.pdf#page=46)

> Engineers should not be expected to learn new things and automate on their off time, this should be baked into their schedule.
>
> [Accelerate at p.71](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/2024_final_dora_report.pdf#page=71)
