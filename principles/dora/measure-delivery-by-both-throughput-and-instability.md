# Measure delivery by both throughput and instability

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Metrics (metrics)

Evidence: measurement

How to measure: Track all five DORA metrics over time and check whether throughput gains come with stable or better change fail and rework rates.

Judge software delivery on how many changes reach production and on how well those deployments go. Track both over time, because a team that gains speed while stability worsens is not actually improving.

## Quotes

> DORA’s research has repeatedly demonstrated that speed and stability are not tradeoffs. In fact, we see that the metrics are correlated for most teams.
>
> [DORA | DORA’s software delivery performance metrics](https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics/)

> Top performers do well across all five metrics, and low performers do poorly.
>
> [DORA | DORA’s software delivery performance metrics](https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics/)
