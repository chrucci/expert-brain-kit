# Judge AI by downstream impact, not adoption

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Metrics (metrics)

Evidence: measurement

How to measure: Compare AI reliance scores with delivery stability, throughput, product performance and well-being instead of reporting adoption or usage counts.

Usage numbers say little about whether AI is helping. Judge it by how much people rely on it for core work and by its effect on people, products and delivery, because process gains alone did not produce better delivery.

## Quotes

> We determined measuring usage frequency is likely not as meaningful as measuring reliance for understanding AI’s centrality to development workflows.
>
> [Accelerate at p.30](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/2024_final_dora_report.pdf#page=30)

> Implement a measurement framework that evaluates AI not by sheer adoption but by meaningful downstream impacts — how it helps employees thrive, benefits those who rely on your products, and unlocks team potential.
>
> [Accelerate at p.46](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/2024_final_dora_report.pdf#page=46)
