# Trust but verify AI output

Verified: 4 verbatim quotes from 2 sources

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Track review speed alongside defects escaping review and the change failure rate on AI-generated changes, before and after validation training.

As work moves to AI, the central human jobs become verification, orchestration and workflow design. Train people to guide, evaluate and validate AI work instead of just pushing usage, and treat faster reviews as a possible sign of over-trust rather than a win.

## Quotes

> Of course, faster code reviews and approvals do not equate to better and more thorough code review processes and approval processes.
>
> [Accelerate at p.38](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/2024_final_dora_report.pdf#page=38)

> It is possible that we’re gaining speed through an over-reliance on AI for assisting in the process or trusting code generated by AI a bit too much.
>
> [Accelerate at p.38](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/2024_final_dora_report.pdf#page=38)
