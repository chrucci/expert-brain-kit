# Remove developer friction rather than optimising AI use

Verified: 3 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Processes (processes)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Compare lead time, deployment frequency and defect rates for teams that invested in friction removal against teams that only rolled out AI tools.

AI coding tools have not visibly improved delivery speed or quality so far. Removing process and tooling friction from developers' daily work gives more, and the AI moment gives leaders the business case to fund it.

## Quotes

> Personally, the lack of impact by AI coding tools to date upon the speed of development and quality of software, is amusing to me.
>
> [Frictionless: why great developer experience can help teams win in the ‘AI age’](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/frictionless-why-great-developer)

> Engineering leaders finally have a case for the resources of time and money to reduce/remove processes, improve tooling, and make developer experience better at both small and large teams.
>
> [Frictionless: why great developer experience can help teams win in the ‘AI age’](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/frictionless-why-great-developer)

> I like Frictionless because it doesn’t talk about how to use AI differently or “better”: instead, it provides suggestions about removing annoying friction from devs’ day-to-day lives.
>
> [Frictionless: why great developer experience can help teams win in the ‘AI age’](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/frictionless-why-great-developer)
