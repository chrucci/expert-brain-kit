# Engineers own areas, architecture and the release decision

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 5 sources

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Track the share of the codebase with a named human owner who approves changes and releases, and compare incident resolution time and parallel-change conflicts in owned and unowned areas.

As agents take over implementation, each engineer owns an area of the system end to end. They hold its architecture, set direction for several parallel agents, answer for whether it works, and decide what goes to production. Ownership should be visible and tied to accountability, not to who typed the code.
