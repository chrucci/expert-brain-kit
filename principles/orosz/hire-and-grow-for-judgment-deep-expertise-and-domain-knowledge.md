# Hire and grow for judgment, deep expertise and domain knowledge

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 6 sources

Dimension: Skills (skills)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Compare agent-assisted delivery quality and feature adoption across engineers by seniority, domain depth and customer exposure, and track whether engineers can defend the design of agent-written code.

AI amplifies the expertise engineers already have, and it helps experts most. As implementation gets cheap, the scarce skills are judgment, systems oversight, taste, and knowing the business and its customers. Teams should keep engineers learning instead of handing the learning to the agent, and hire for these skills over raw coding speed.
