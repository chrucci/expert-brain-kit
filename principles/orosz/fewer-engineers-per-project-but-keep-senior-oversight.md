# Fewer engineers per project, but keep senior oversight

Verified: 7 verbatim quotes from 3 sources

Dimension: Number of people (team-size)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Track engineers per project against throughput and merge conflicts, and senior headcount on critical teams against incident frequency and time to detect problems.

Small, well-skilled teams with agents can do far more, and projects need fewer humans because too many engineers running agents get in each other's way. Teams still own software and on-call. Drastic reorganisations that strip senior infra and security staff remove the people who oversee agent output.
