# Measure delivery outcomes, not AI activity

Verified: 8 verbatim quotes from 6 sources

Dimension: Metrics (metrics)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Track change failure rate and regressions together with deployment frequency and lead time against a pre-AI baseline, and check whether activity metrics such as tokens or acceptance correlate with those outcomes.

Acceptance rate, AI-generated lines and token counts reward looking busy, and token-based reviews push engineers to burn tokens. Judge AI by a balanced set of outcome metrics against a pre-rollout baseline, pairing speed with reliability. Expect gains to show up as saved time unless incentives change, and check real speed against how fast the team feels.
