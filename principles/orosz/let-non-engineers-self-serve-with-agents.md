# Let non-engineers self-serve with agents

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Roles for humans (human-roles)

Evidence: experience

How to measure: Count escalations and interruptions from non-engineering teams to engineers per week before and after those teams adopt agents.

When marketing, support and other teams use agents to do work that used to depend on engineers, escalations and interruptions fall and engineers get time for deeper work. Adoption outside engineering is an engineering productivity lever.

## Quotes

> Marketing used to have a hard dependence on engineering to build websites, but now they do it on their own!
>
> [Inside a five-year-old startup’s rapid AI makeover](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/ai-first-makeover-craft)

> Also, customer support no longer has to escalate to engineering; instead, an agent does it automatically. The outcome is fewer interruptions and not missing customer complaints.
>
> [Inside a five-year-old startup’s rapid AI makeover](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/ai-first-makeover-craft)
