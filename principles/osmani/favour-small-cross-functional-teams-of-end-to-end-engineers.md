# Favour small cross-functional teams of end-to-end engineers

Verified: 2 verbatim quotes from 1 source

Dimension: Number of people (team-size)

Evidence: opinion

How to measure: Compare delivery throughput and quality between small cross-functional teams with end-to-end ownership and larger specialised teams.

With AI help, one engineer covers more ground, so teams do best when they are small and cross-functional, staffed by engineers with deep expertise in one or two areas and broad literacy elsewhere.

## Quotes

> Developers with the deepest knowledge in one or two areas while maintaining broad literacy tend to deliver the best results.
>
> [Earning taste and judgment](https://addyosmani.com/blog/earning-judgment/)

> Teams that lean into small cross-functional teams and end-to-end engineers will thrive.
>
> [Earning taste and judgment](https://addyosmani.com/blog/earning-judgment/)
